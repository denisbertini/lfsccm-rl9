## Architecture decision final
**All-in-one Lima VM**: Single Rocky 9.8 VM runs Lustre MDS+OSS server, client view at `/mnt/lustre`, Plus Docker Compose multi-node cluster, zero pollution on the Linux Host. Each container gets `-v /mnt/lustre:/lustre`. Swappable via slurm.conf. Two plugins side-by-side available: legacy PCC + lustre_mpc native C plugin. Swap between them using `BB_PLUGIN` env var in entrypoint.sh
